// About

We build people,
not just campaigns.

Cross Point Consulting is a Charlotte-based B2B client acquisition team — part of a national organization that grew from one office in 1999 to 75 across the country.

// Origin

It started in 1999 with one office and one belief: the best leaders are the ones who've done the work themselves.

Two decades later that belief had built a national footprint — 75 offices, with residential, retail, and business-to-business teams. The Charlotte office is where we focus on B2B: representing Fortune 500 brands and winning new business face to face.

What never changed is how we grow. We don't hire managers from outside. Everyone starts at entry level, because we want leaders who've personally done everything they'll one day teach. No seniority. 100% promotion from within.
