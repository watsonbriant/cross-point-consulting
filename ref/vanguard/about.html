Cross Point Consulting is a Charlotte-based business-to-business client acquisition team — part of a national organization that grew from a single office in 1999 to 75 across the country.

Our story

It started in 1999 with one office and one belief: the best leaders are the ones who've done the work themselves.

Over the next two decades that belief built a national footprint — 75 offices across the United States, with residential, retail, and business-to-business teams. The Charlotte office is where we focus on B2B: representing Fortune 500 brands and winning new business face to face.

What never changed is how we grow. We don't hire managers from the outside. Everyone — without exception — starts at the entry level, because we want our leaders to have personally done everything they'll one day teach. No seniority. 100% promotion from within.
